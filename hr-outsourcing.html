Why outsource

Your managers should be managing work, not paperwork

In South Sudan, weak HR administration is expensive. A missed tax remittance brings penalties. An undocumented dismissal becomes a labour dispute. Unrecorded overtime becomes a wage claim. We remove those risks and give you clean records you can rely on.

  • Lower cost than an in-house HR team
  • Compliance with the Labour Act 2017
  • Accurate, on-time payroll every month
  • Audit-ready records and reports
  • Fewer disputes and grievances
  • Scale up or down with your projects

Labour management

Control of your workforce on every site, every day

Labour management is the operational side of HR: making sure the right number of workers are on site, paid correctly, working safely and treated fairly. It matters most for construction, oil and gas, logistics and humanitarian operations with large, changing workforces. We manage it for you, from mobilisation to demobilisation.

Workforce planning & mobilisation

We forecast labour needs against your project schedule, recruit to plan and mobilise workers to site with documentation complete.

Time, attendance & rosters

Daily attendance registers, shift and rotation rosters, and overtime records verified before payroll, so you only pay for work done.

Casual & daily-rated workers

Registration, ID and contracts for casual labour, fair daily rates, and weekly or monthly pay runs with signed payment records.

Site induction & workplace rules

Induction on site rules, conduct and safety expectations, with signed acknowledgements kept on each worker's file.

Labour relations & community hiring

Fair local hiring quotas agreed with communities, worker representatives consulted, and grievances resolved before they stop work.

Labour office liaison

We handle notifications and correspondence with labour authorities and represent you in labour matters.

Headcount & cost reporting

Weekly or monthly reports on headcount, attendance, labour cost and turnover by site, for management and donors.

Demobilisation & exits

Planned demobilisation at project end, with final pay, end-of-service entitlements and clearance handled correctly.
