HR Advisory Services

The HR partner you need to thrive

Talent gaps drain productivity and compliance weighs you down. We build the HR foundations that let organisations grow: policies that hold up, structures that make sense and people who know what's expected of them.

  • HR audits that show where practice falls short of law and good practice.
  • Complete HR policy suites: HR manual, recruitment, remuneration, performance, discipline, exits and employee handbook.
  • Job descriptions that attract top talent, with clarity on role, expectations and fit.
  • Skill-gap analysis and targeted corporate training programmes.
Taxation Services

Stay on the right side of the law

Tax compliance is the backbone of sustainable growth, yet South Sudan's regulations can overwhelm even committed businesses. Missed filings and weak documentation trigger costly penalties. Our leadership brings experience from the Australian Taxation Office to simplify the path.

  • Business profit tax and income tax filings, payroll and withholding compliance.
  • Tax compliance reviews, audit readiness and support during assessments.
  • Complete financial statements when you need them.
